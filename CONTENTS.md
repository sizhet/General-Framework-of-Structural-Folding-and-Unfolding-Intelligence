## 1. Repository Purpose

GFSFUI studies a general Structural Intelligence lifecycle:

> **Represent → Fold → Localize → Search → Challenge → Unfold → Validate → Certify → Grow → Refold**

The repository is organized around seven core papers, seven canonical figures, and a small set of navigation and release files.

# 18. Canonical Research Thesis

The broad GFSFUI thesis is:

> **Intelligence can be organized around the continuous movement between concrete individuals and reusable structural knowledge.**

Folding moves toward reusable structure.

Search activates and challenges that structure.

Unfolding projects it toward new goals.

Validation determines what should become trusted memory.

Refolding turns validated results into future intelligence.

---

# 19. Repository Scope

GFSFUI is intended as:

> **a general engineering and research framework**

It is not presented as:

* a completed theory of general intelligence,
* one universal metric,
* one fixed tree architecture,
* one ontology,
* one coding model,
* one medical model,
* or one universal runtime.

The framework is deliberately open to future structural Delta.
