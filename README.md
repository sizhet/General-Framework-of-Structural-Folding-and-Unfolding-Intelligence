# General Framework of Structural Folding and Unfolding Intelligence — GFSFUI

> **Fold experience into structure. Search where the structure holds and where it fails. Unfold structural memory toward new goals. Validate the result, and fold the new experience back into a growing intelligence.**

---

## 1. What Is GFSFUI?

**GFSFUI — General Framework of Structural Folding and Unfolding Intelligence** is a general structural intelligence framework for organizing how concrete experience becomes reusable structural memory, how that memory is searched and challenged, and how it can later be unfolded toward new goals.

The canonical lifecycle is:

```text
Represent
   ↓
Fold
   ↓
Localize
   ↓
Search
   ↓
Challenge
   ↓
Unfold
   ↓
Validate
   ↓
Certify
   ↓
Grow
   ↓
Refold
```

GFSFUI is not a claim of a completed universal theory of intelligence.

It is a general engineering and research framework built around a simple question:

> **How can intelligence continuously move between many concrete individuals and reusable structural knowledge?**

---

## 2. The Central Idea

Many intelligent systems repeatedly operate directly on large collections of concrete data.

GFSFUI proposes another path:

```text
Many Concrete Individuals
        ↓
Structural Representation
        ↓
Structural Folding
        ↓
Reusable Structural Memory
        ↓
Localized Search and Intelligence
        ↓
Goal-Conditioned Unfolding
        ↓
New Concrete Structure
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Refolding
```

The goal is not merely to compress data.

The goal is to preserve:

* reusable Core structure,
* meaningful Delta,
* structural neighborhoods,
* Cluster-Central CCCs,
* structural DNA,
* context,
* provenance,
* evidence,
* counter-evidence,
* local intelligence,
* and structural growth paths.

## 3. The Three General Mechanisms

GFSFUI begins with three general mechanisms.

### 3.1 Generic Structural Representation

A concrete individual is transformed into a:

> **GenericContainerStarmap**

The Starmap provides a common structural interface for domain-specific objects.

Examples may include:

* stock-market sequences,
* biomedical observations,
* CallingGraphs,
* behavioral trajectories,
* program traces,
* machine states,
* graphs,
* plans,
* or other structurally comparable objects.

The key idea is:

> **Domain-specific representation at the edge; domain-independent structural intelligence in the core.**

---

### 3.2 Generic Structural Folding

Structurally represented individuals are compared through one or more meaningful metrics or structural similarity functions.

The canonical transformation is:

```text
Similarity
    ↓
Shared Structure
    ↓
Difference Localization
    ↓
Dispatch
```

A Metric Differential structure can then organize:

* structural neighborhoods,
* Cluster-Central CCCs,
* Core,
* Delta,
* structural DNA,
* and reusable local intelligence.

The tree is a useful canonical implementation, but the deeper concept is a:

> **Metric Differential Structure**

---

### 3.3 Generic Per-Node Intelligence

A structural node can host intelligence appropriate to its local region.

Examples include:

* CCC,
* DNA,
* rules,
* probability models,
* ANN,
* LLM specialists,
* domain algorithms,
* search policies,
* validation policies,
* user plugins,
* or human review.

Thus:

> **The tree localizes intelligence; it does not monopolize intelligence.**

## 5. Structural Search Plane

Once structural memory exists, search should not be limited to raw files or raw individuals.

GFSFUI introduces the:

> **Structural Search Plane**

A canonical cross-layer view is:

```text
Raw Individual
      ↕
GenericContainerStarmap
      ↕
Metric Structure
      ↕
Tree / Node
      ↕
Cluster-Central CCC
      ↕
Structural DNA
      ↕
Dispatch / Trigger
      ↕
Outcome
      ↕
Evidence / Counter-Evidence
      ↕
Provenance
```

Structural Search has at least four roles:

1. **Retrieval**
2. **Verification**
3. **Discovery**
4. **Growth**

---

## 6. Two-Way Structural Search

Many structural relations should be searchable in both directions.

For example:

```text
DNA → Outcome
```

and:

```text
Outcome → DNA
```

The two directions answer different questions.

Together they provide a stronger structural test than one-way association alone.

Two-Way Search can operate across:

* Individual ↔ CCC
* Node ↔ DNA
* DNA ↔ Outcome
* Trigger ↔ Outcome
* Core ↔ Delta
* CallingGraph ↔ Bigram / Trigram
* Identity ↔ Structural Role

---

## 7. Counter-Evidence and Structural Falsification

A structural intelligence system should not only search for supporting evidence.

It should explicitly ask:

> **Where does the current Fold fail?**

This is:

> **Structural Falsification**

A contradiction may indicate:

* noise,
* a missing condition,
* a hidden subgroup,
* a weak metric,
* an incomplete Starmap,
* a context difference,
* or a genuinely new structure.

The critical idea is:

> **Counter-evidence does not merely lower a score; it can create a structural Delta.**

A canonical growth chain is:

```text
Counter-Evidence
      ↓
Delta
      ↓
Candidate CCC
      ↓
Local A/B
      ↓
Branch
      ↓
Specialist
      ↓
Brain Unit
```

---

## 8. Continual Structural Learning

GFSFUI treats continual learning as localized structural growth.

A canonical loop is:

```text
New Observation
      ↓
Structural Dispatch
      ↓
Existing Node
      ↓
Consistency Check
      ↓
Support or Difference
      ↓
Candidate Delta
      ↓
Local Validation
      ↓
Reinforce or Grow
```

This suggests:

> **Continual Structural Learning = Continual Folding + Structural Search + Counter-Evidence + Localized Growth**

The architectural objective is:

> **Growth is explicit, addressable, inspectable, and locally certifiable.**

---

## 9. 3-Cat Structural Learning

Sparse-data intelligence is one of the most important consequences of the framework.

A few high-information examples may be sufficient to create a provisional Fold.

But:

> **Few examples may be sufficient to initiate a Fold; they are not necessarily sufficient to certify it.**

The canonical process is:

```text
Few Examples
     ↓
Candidate Fold
     ↓
Candidate CCC / DNA
     ↓
Structural Search
     ↓
Support + Counter-Evidence
     ↓
Delta
     ↓
Validation
     ↓
Certification / Rejection
     ↓
Growth
     ↓
Refold
```

The purpose is not to claim that three examples prove a general rule.

The purpose is to allow intelligence to begin organizing sparse evidence while remaining explicitly falsifiable.

---

## 10. Structural Unfolding

Structural Folding moves from concrete experience toward reusable structure.

Structural Unfolding moves from reusable structure toward a new goal.

Conceptually:

```text
Fold:
Concrete → Structural

Unfold:
Structural + Goal + Context → New Concrete Structure
```

The two are complementary rather than exact inverses.

A useful Fold should preserve enough structure to support future Unfolding.

A useful Unfold should remain testable, certifiable, and Refoldable.

---

A mature program can produce a CallingGraph.

A population of mature programs therefore produces a population of Foldable CallingGraphs.

```text

CallingGraph Population
      ↓
GenericContainerStarmap
      ↓
Structural Folding
      ↓
CG CCC / DNA / Core ± Delta
      ↓
Structural Search

      ↓
CallingGraph Unfolding
      ↓
Candidate CG
      ↓
Counter-Evidence
      ↓
Validation / Execution
      ↓
Certification
      ↓
Refold
```

The central recursive insight is:

> **A CallingGraph is an Unfolding product at one stage and a Folding individual at the next stage.**

## 12. Bigram and Trigram CG DNA

CallingGraph paths can be decomposed into lightweight structural relations.

For:

```text
A → B → C → D
```

Bigrams include:

```text
A → B
B → C
C → D
```

Trigrams include:

```text
A → B → C
B → C → D
```

After local role normalization, these relations can become reusable CallingGraph DNA.

This provides an inexpensive intermediate structural layer before whole-path or whole-graph comparison.

## 13. Local UTN and Identity

Fold–Unfold intelligence requires comparable identities.

But requiring a completed universal naming system before Folding begins creates a bootstrapping problem.

GFSFUI therefore uses the principle:

> **Localize first. Universalize progressively.**

The progression is:

```text
Raw Identity
      ↓
Local Identity
      ↓
Local UTN
      ↓
Structural Role
      ↓
Candidate Cross-Context Equivalence
      ↓
Two-Way Search
      ↓
Counter-Evidence
      ↓
Certified Identity
      ↓
Optional Universal UTN
```

The core principle is:

> **Identity precedes Naming.**

---

## 14. Context-Preserving Unification

A useful Fold should not destroy the local context that makes an identity meaningful.

The intended relation is:

```text
Local Identity
      ↓
Shared Core

Context
      +
Delta
      +
Provenance
```

Therefore:

> **Fold does not delete Context; it separates reusable Core from recoverable Context.**

# 36. What GFSFUI Does Not Claim

GFSFUI does not claim that:

* one metric solves all structural comparison;
* one tree captures all useful intelligence;
* structural similarity proves causality;
* sparse examples automatically generalize;
* CallingGraphs capture all software semantics;
* Local UTN solves universal ontology alignment;
* counter-evidence search always finds every contradiction;
* local growth always replaces global learning;
* structural search eliminates LLMs;
* or this repository presents a completed theory of general intelligence.

GFSFUI is a general research framework designed to make these questions more explicit, testable, and structurally connected.

# 37. Explicit Leftover and Policy Governance

GFSFUI does **not** require every object, query, or candidate to be forced into an existing structural branch.

At major structural stages, the runtime may preserve an explicit **Leftover** branch for noise, uncertainty, outliers, novelty, insufficient evidence, or deferred processing:

$$
X
=
Folded(X)
\cup
Leftover(X)
$$

Leftover is therefore not necessarily failure. It may represent the current boundary of reliable structural coverage and provide future material for Difference discovery, new branches, new CCCs, and structural growth.

GFSFUI also distinguishes structural evidence from runtime action:

$$
\boxed{
Structural\ Evidence
\neq
Policy
\neq
Decision
}
$$

Structural intelligence defines the available possibility space. Policy governs acceptance thresholds, search width, unfolding budgets, validation requirements, escalation, Leftover handling, and structural-growth decisions.

Together:

$$
\boxed{
Structural\ Space
+
Leftover\ Space
+
Policy\ Space
}
$$

provide a more realistic foundation for controllable Fold/Unfold intelligence.

For the full treatment, see:

**GFSFUI-008 — Explicit Leftover and Policy-Governed Structural Runtime**

# 38. The Research Thesis

The broad thesis is:

> **Intelligence can be organized around the continuous movement between concrete individuals and reusable structural knowledge.**

Folding moves from experience toward structure.

Search activates and challenges that structure.

Unfolding projects structure toward a new goal.

Validation determines what should become trusted experience.

Refolding turns the result into future intelligence.

> **Metric Differential structures Fold populations into reusable structural memory.**
