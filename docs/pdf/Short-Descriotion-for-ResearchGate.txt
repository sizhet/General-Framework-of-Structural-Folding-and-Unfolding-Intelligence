The Structural Folding Front studies how concrete experience can be Folded into reusable structural memory and selectively Unfolded under new goals. Its three coordinated DOI repositories progress from application to runtime to general framework: SMSF explores Structural Folding through stock-market experience; CSFR develops reusable CCC folding, localization, DNA dispatch, and structural-search mechanisms; and GFSFUI generalizes Fold, Search, Unfold, Validate, Grow, Refold, Leftover, and Policy into a broader Structural Intelligence lifecycle. Full research records: SMSF — https://github.com/sizhet/Stock-Market-Structural-Folding-SMSF ; CSFR — https://github.com/sizhet/CCC-Structural-Folding-Runtime-CSFR ; GFSFUI — https://github.com/sizhet/General-Framework-of-Structural-Folding-and-Unfolding-Intelligence
